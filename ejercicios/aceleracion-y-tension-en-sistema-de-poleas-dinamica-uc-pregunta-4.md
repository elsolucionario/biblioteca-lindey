When the blocks are released, determine their acceleration and the tension in the cable. Neglect the mass of the pulleys.

Figure data: block A (30 kg) hangs from a movable pulley; the cable is fixed to the ceiling, wraps around the movable pulley, goes up over a fixed pulley on the ceiling and down to block B (40 kg).
